    Learn the puzzle

    How to play

    Tap a square to cycle Empty → Tent → Grass. Use Grass marks to rule out cells as you solve.

    Rules

    Every tree needs one orthogonally adjacent tent. Tents cannot touch another tent horizontally, vertically, or diagonally.

    Tips

    Start with trees that have only one possible neighboring square, then use rows and columns that are already full.

    FAQ

    Questions about Tents and Trees

    Can tents touch diagonally?

    No. A tent cannot touch another tent in any of the eight surrounding squares.

    Is Practice unlimited?

    Yes. Practice creates as many free puzzles as you want without an account.
