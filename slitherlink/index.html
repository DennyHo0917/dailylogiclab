Goal

    Click an edge to cycle Unknown → Line → X. Every number is exact.

    Learn the puzzle

    How to play

    Tap an edge once for a line, twice for an X, and again to clear it. Use X marks to keep the loop clean.

    Rules

    Each number tells you how many of its four sides belong to the final loop. The finished loop is continuous, closed, and never branches.

    Tips

    A 0 has four X edges. A 3 has three lines. At every vertex, two lines must eventually meet.

    FAQ

    Questions about Slitherlink

    What does each number mean?

    It is the exact number of loop edges around that cell.

    Can the loop have a branch?

    No. Every used vertex in the final loop has exactly two line edges.
